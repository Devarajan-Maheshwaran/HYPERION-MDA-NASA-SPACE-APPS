# HYPERION — Research Bibliography

Links checked against official NASA or Canadian Space Agency pages on 14 November 2026. A live URL is not proof that a dataset is suitable for this project; inspect the individual record, metadata, access conditions, and license before use.

## NASA human-spaceflight research

1. **NASA Human Research Program — Five Hazards of Human Spaceflight**  
   https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/  
   Describes radiation, isolation/confinement, distance from Earth, gravity fields, and hostile/closed environments. Use for the problem framing. It is a hazard taxonomy, not a clinical algorithm.

2. **NASA — Risks of Human Spaceflight**  
   https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/  
   Overview of specific human-system risks. Risk applicability varies with mission and exposure; avoid flattening all risks into universal symptoms.

3. **NASA ExMC, ExMC CDS Use Cases for CDSS, HRP-48069 Rev. B**  
   https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20220017885/downloads/HRP-48069%20ExMC%20CDS%20Use%20Cases%20Doc%20Rev.%20B%20-%20MK%20BB%20BE%20AM%20signed.pdf  
   NASA technical use-case document for exploration medical clinical decision support. Useful architecture/workflow precedent. Its scenarios are not a validated model, medical guideline for HYPERION, or approval of this project.

4. **NASA-STD-3001, Space Flight Human-System Standard**  
   https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo/human-spaceflight-and-aviation-standards/  
   Official NASA page linking Volume 1 (Crew Health) and Volume 2 (Human Factors, Habitability, and Environmental Health); consult the current revision and applicability information. A standard is not a source of universal diagnostic cutoffs. Do not claim compliance without formal requirements traceability and applicable review.

5. **NASA Spaceflight Standard Measures**  
   https://ntrs.nasa.gov/search?q=Spaceflight%20Standard%20Measures  
   NASA Technical Reports Server search for standard-measures publications. Select the specific document and inspect measures, definitions, collection time points, and caveats; this search is not itself a dataset.

6. **NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)**  
   https://osdr.nasa.gov/  
   NASA repository for space biology/human research data and study records. Search the collection and inspect each study’s metadata, permissions, variable dictionary, and cohort. Not every dataset concerns astronauts or supports health prediction.

7. **NASA Human Research Roadmap — Risks**  
   https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/  
   Risk portfolio and evidence context. Content and portfolio organization may evolve; cite the specific risk page/document used.

8. **NASA software engineering requirements (NPR 7150.2D)**  
   https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/software-management-office/nasa-software-engineering-procedural-requirements-standards-and-related-resources/  
   NASA Software Management Office page identifies the current procedural requirements. Verify applicability; referencing it does not establish compliance.

9. **NASA software assurance and software safety standard (NASA-STD-8739.8)**  
   https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-87398  
   Official standards catalog entry. Verify active revision and applicability; do not claim project compliance from citation alone.

10. **NASA CIPHER (Complement of Integrated Protocols for Human Exploration Research)**  
    https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/  
    Start from NASA HRP and locate CIPHER study materials/publications. CIPHER is a research program/protocol context, not a guaranteed public labeled dataset or a plug-in model-training corpus. Cite a specific study record when making a concrete claim.

## Canadian Space Agency

11. **CSA — Bio-Monitor: Keeping an eye on astronauts’ vital signs**  
    https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/bio-monitor.asp  
    Official description of wearable garment, headband, and tablet application used to record multiple physiological measures in ISS context. Does not provide an open integration API or establish HYPERION compatibility.

12. **CSA — The Space Health study**  
    https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/space-flight-improve-monitor-health-earth.asp  
    Describes research using Bio-Monitor measurements and the Artemis analytics platform. Distinguish research analysis on Earth from an onboard real-time diagnostic function.

13. **CSA — Vascular series**  
    https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/vascular.asp  
    Background on cardiovascular research in space, including Bio-Monitor context. It is research background, not validated decision thresholds.

## Source-use caveats
- Prefer the specific official report or dataset record over a general program page.
- Record access date, revision/version, study ID, units, variables, and license in any analysis.
- Do not assume public access means unrestricted reuse or redistribution.
- Do not combine measurements from distinct instruments/units without a documented transformation.
- A cited standard or NASA/CSA system is not NASA/CSA endorsement of HYPERION.
