# HYPERION-MDA — project brief

## Purpose
Explore a local software workflow for organizing physiological telemetry, showing data quality and personal trends, and making uncertainty visible. The name expands to “Hybrid Privacy-Preserving Epistemic Risk & Integrated Onboard Medical Data Architecture.” This is a proposed student/research prototype concept, not an operational clinical system.

## User problem
Long-duration missions involve changing environments and mission workloads, asynchronous measurements, limited communication, and sensitive health information. Software could help organize and contextualize data, but software cannot establish a diagnosis simply by detecting a statistical deviation.

## Proposed pillars
1. **Telemetry and data quality:** ingest supported records with units, timestamps, source, and quality metadata.
2. **Personal/contextual analysis:** compare measurements with an individual's documented history and available activity context; expose uncertainty.
3. **Guided decision support:** present traceable workflow steps for human review. Protocol content must come from qualified, authoritative sources and receive appropriate review.

## Four gaps described in the concept material
- Non-stationary physiology across mission contexts.
- Mission workload/environment effects can confound interpretation.
- Mixed-rate continuous and episodic observations.
- Small cohorts and privacy constraints complicate pooled analysis.
