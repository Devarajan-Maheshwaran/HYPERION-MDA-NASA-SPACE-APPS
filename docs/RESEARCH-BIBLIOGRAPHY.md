# Research bibliography and caveats

Official NASA/CSA pages checked 14 November 2026. Confirm document revision and study-specific metadata before relying on a source.

1. NASA Human Research Program, **Five Hazards of Human Spaceflight** — https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/ . Hazard framework, not a clinical algorithm.
2. NASA, **Risks of Human Spaceflight** — https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/ . Risk portfolio; mission-specific applicability varies.
3. NASA ExMC, **ExMC CDS Use Cases for CDSS, HRP-48069 Rev. B** — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20220017885/downloads/HRP-48069%20ExMC%20CDS%20Use%20Cases%20Doc%20Rev.%20B%20-%20MK%20BB%20BE%20AM%20signed.pdf . Engineering use cases, not HYPERION validation or treatment authority.
4. NASA Human Spaceflight and Aviation Standards, **NASA-STD-3001 Volumes 1 and 2** — https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo/human-spaceflight-and-aviation-standards/ . Use current revisions and applicable requirements; citation alone is not compliance.
5. NASA Open Science Data Repository — https://osdr.nasa.gov/ . Inspect individual study, cohort, variables, terms, and labels; repository contents are not automatically suitable for this use.
6. NASA Human Research Roadmap risks — https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/ . Portfolio context; cite the particular risk item used.
7. NASA Software Engineering Requirements and Standards — https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/software-management-office/nasa-software-engineering-procedural-requirements-standards-and-related-resources/ . Engineering reference, not a claim of compliance.
8. NASA-STD-8739.8, **Software Assurance and Software Safety Standard** — https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-87398 . Verify active revision/applicability; no compliance is claimed here.
9. Canadian Space Agency, **Bio-Monitor: Keeping an eye on astronauts' vital signs** — https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/bio-monitor.asp . Related wearable system; does not establish available API or HYPERION compatibility.
10. CSA, **The Space Health study** — https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/space-flight-improve-monitor-health-earth.asp . Research context; distinguish ground-based analysis from onboard decision support.
11. CSA, **Vascular series** — https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/vascular.asp . Cardiovascular research context, not a set of decision cutoffs.

The pasted HYPERION-MDA concept mentions NASA OSDR OSD-570, RADLab, WESAD, CIPHER, CSA dosimetry/experiments, and various specific measurements. Those references must be matched to exact study records and verified before assigning their data to this architecture. Do not infer access or licensing from a program name.
